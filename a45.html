Object-Fit & Object-Position

Different ways to control how images fit inside a container.

object-fit: cover

Fills the container and crops excess content.

object-fit: contain

Shows the complete image inside the container.

object-fit: fill

Stretches the image to fill the container.

object-fit: none

Keeps the image at its original size.

object-position: top

Positions the image toward the top.

object-position: bottom

Positions the image toward the bottom.

object-position: left

Positions the image toward the left.

object-position: right

Positions the image toward the right.
